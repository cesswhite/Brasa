# Story inside the world of Brasa

Integrated redesign of the five sections of Story Mode. Godot continues to control text, navigation, statistics, progress and actions; seven original images provide the settings, surfaces and objects. The pre-implementation audit and plan is at [UI-WORLD-PLAN.md](UI-WORLD-PLAN.md).

## before and after

| Element | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route | Flat background and grid of cards before the opponent's piece | Illustrated environment, protagonist rival and path of connected milestones |
| Chapters and bosses | Almost identical presentation | Lanterns or roots and storm; Bosses with larger scale, emblem and contextual light |
| Upgrades and Movements | Stats and Technique Cards | Training workshop, open queues, character and native investment controls |
| Companions | Card roster and secondary description | Refugio, selected companion in large format and squad below |
| Legacy | Uniform data blocks | Illustrated file, medallion and memories with data from each chapter |
| Buttons and frames | Flat rectangles generated by code | Family of leather, fabric and copper that adapts through nine segments |
| Small navigation | Compact controls without their own language | Straps from the same atlas, without finishes that invade the mobile labels |
| Decoration | No contextual belongings | Up to three items chosen based on body, cosmetics, completed encounters and League victories |
| Adaptive composition | Small rival after the map | Rival next to the text on the desktop, vertically on top and always accessible actions |

## Art and system

Five backgrounds: Lanterns, Storm, Workshop, Shelter and Archive. Two transparent atlases: surfaces/badges and objects. The original PNGs are preserved; regions and styles live in [ui_visual_manifest.json](../data/ui_visual_manifest.json). All labels, numbers, and values ​​remain native text.

The manifest offers eleven reusable contexts, including boss variants per chapter. Later chapters reuse Storm's environment with a different chromatic treatment; This release does not create eleven unique funds. Improvements and Movements share the workshop in a different light.

Objects use declarative conditions and priorities. Tepa uniform and Balam headbands follow the equipped body. The brazier is reminiscent of Ascua; the relic requires progress and its cosmetic; the backpack accompanies the trip; a medal appears after ten League victories. Items ignore mouse/focus and do not grant rewards.

Only the background of the current section and the two shared atlases are retained. The previous fund release was verified with weak references. No particles, shaders or environmental animation were added. Normal, hover, down, focus, and disabled states remain native, with fallback if an essential surface is missing.

ChatGPT's built-in image tool was used. It does not expose a version selector "2.5"; that version is not attributed to the result. [GENERATION-PROMPTS.json](../assets/ui/GENERATION-PROMPTS.json) contains the complete seven prompts, destinations, and hashes. The [art direction](../assets/ui/ART-DIRECTION.md) describes materials, light and integration criteria.

## Validation

Final result: **3.824 checks in nine runs, zero crashes and zero engine errors**, with 63 native Story Mode captures. The seven generated PNGs retain their original hashes, as do the seven source and backup files recorded at startup.

1360×880, 1224×792, 1920×1080, 768×1024, 390×844, 430×932 and 844×390 were checked. Revised Chapter Rival 2, Boss, Starting Route, Ten Encounter Chapters, Completed File, and All Five Sections. In small formats the body moves vertically; the foot retains its actions. The full text of long milestone names is available in its preview and tooltip.

Regressions cover point allocation, techniques, talents, partner selection, retry, practice, combat entry, chapter change, legacy, identity, persistence, and return to League. The final evidence and figures per suite remain in [ui-world-validation.json](ui-world-validation.json).

The decorative layer does not change rules, XP, rewards, or save format. `main.gd` only adds the pace of team victories in view. The hashes of the game modules and the seven starting and backup files recorded before the change were compared.

The scope is Story Mode and its reusable visual system. The combat arena, character editor and online interface retain their current presentation.
